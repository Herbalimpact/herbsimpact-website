## What Is Haiiba Timam Tea?

Haiiba Timam Tea is a herbal tea developed at **Herbal Impact**, a traditional medicine clinic in Dar es Salaam licensed since 2012 by Tanzania's Traditional and Alternative Health Practice Council. It was developed by Juma Killaghai, a natural products research chemist since 1995 and a licensed traditional medicine practitioner.

The tea is made **from edible plants only**: the kind of plants people already eat as food. You brew it at home in a thermos flask, just like ordinary tea.

## How Long Patients Usually Use It

Most patients use the tea for **2 to 5 weeks**. Chronic headaches usually take about 2 weeks; urinary incontinence and tinnitus about 3 weeks; high blood pressure, joint pain and hormone imbalance about 5 weeks. Back pain, liver disease and vitiligo take around 12 weeks, and certain cancers around 24 weeks. Long-term conditions such as weak immunity, diabetes complications and asthma call for long-term use.

One 200 g packet lasts **7 days**, so a 5-week course needs 5 packets.

## How to Prepare Haiiba Timam Tea

![Woman drinking herbal tea from a yellow mug](/assets/uploads/herbal-tea-woman-yellow-mug.jpg "Photo: Kampus Production / Pexels")

1. Use a small thermos flask that holds 2–3 cups.
2. Put one tablespoon of Haiiba Timam Tea into the flask.
3. Boil 3 cups of water, pour it into the flask and close the lid tightly.
4. Leave it to steep for 30 minutes.
5. Drink all of it within 4 hours.

Prepare and drink it **twice a day**, morning and evening. Take the morning cup on an empty stomach, before breakfast. The evening cup can be taken before or after a meal.
