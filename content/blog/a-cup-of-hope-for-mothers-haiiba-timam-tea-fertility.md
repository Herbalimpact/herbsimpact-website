*Why a doctor friend left his job for childless mothers, and what our clinic has seen since 2012*

A friend of mine, a doctor trained in conventional medicine, told me he had left his job. He now spends his time helping women who cannot get pregnant. Two experiences pushed him to make that choice.

## Two Stories That Changed a Doctor's Path

Years earlier he was the head doctor at a hospital that served one of the country's security forces. Two of his patients there had advanced cancer.

The first was surrounded by his children and other young people who cared for him day and night. They came and went, washed him and kept him company. Despite his pain, he was clean, calm and comforted.

The second had nobody. Apart from the nurses and doctors, no one came. Late-stage cancer can cause a strong smell, and with no one to bathe him or help with daily care, his suffering was plain to see. Some colleagues wanted to discharge him. My friend refused: caring for him was their duty. While my friend was away on a work trip, the patient was discharged anyway. Weak, he walked slowly from the hospital, sat down under a tree to rest, and died there. Soldiers passing by found him and reported it to the hospital.

The lesson my friend took was simple. When you are very sick and have no one to stand up for you, life can become unbearably hard.

The second experience came from a woman who told him she had spent more than **30 million shillings** at a national hospital trying to conceive. Each visit brought a new bill: two million here, more there. She never became pregnant.

That was when he decided to give his life to helping women have children, so that in old age they would have people to care for them. His very first patient was that same woman. For about **300,000 shillings**, she became pregnant.

## What We Have Seen at Herbal Impact

The tea is made from edible plants only and is brewed at home like ordinary tea. It was developed by Juma Killaghai, a natural products research chemist since 1995. It touches many areas of health, but this article focuses on the problems mothers bring to us most often.

## Women's Health Problems Our Patients Have Reported Improving

- **Difficulty getting pregnant.** Women who had tried for years, some of whom believed they would never have children, have conceived after using the tea.
- **Pregnancies that do not hold.** Mothers who kept losing pregnancies, or whose pregnancies stopped developing, report carrying to full term.
- **Ovarian cysts and other growths.** Many were told at hospital that they needed surgery they could not afford. Many report that within a few weeks of using the tea the problem had cleared and they felt well.
- **Bleeding outside the monthly period.** The tea has helped mothers with frequent bleeding, including from small mushroom-like growths near the cervix that bleed when touched. Older women with sudden bleeding of unknown cause have also reported relief.

These are reports from our patients, not results of clinical trials, and results vary from woman to woman.

## Before You Start: Please Read This

![Pregnant woman smiling and holding her belly on a sofa](/assets/uploads/mothers-hope-pregnant-woman.jpg "Photo: SHVETS production / Pexels")

The tea works best **alongside** hospital care, not instead of it.

- **Get a diagnosis first.** An ultrasound and a doctor's check tell you what you are dealing with and let you see your progress.
- **Bleeding after menopause, or any sudden unexplained bleeding, must be checked at a hospital first** to rule out cancer.
- **Severe pain, heavy bleeding or fainting**, including during pregnancy, means going to a hospital the same day.
- **Do not cancel a planned operation** until a new scan and your doctor confirm the problem has cleared.
- **Once you are pregnant,** keep drinking the tea. From our experience, continuing is an advantage, especially for mothers prone to miscarriage. Tell your antenatal clinic you are drinking it and keep every antenatal visit.

## "But Aren't Herbs Bad for the Kidneys?"

Some people fear herbal remedies, believing they damage the kidneys or other organs. We understand that fear. Our view, after more than 30 years in natural products research, is that edible plants are natural materials the body already knows as food. That is why Haiiba Timam Tea uses edible plants only. From our experience since 2012, women of all ages have used it safely. If you take other medicines, tell your doctor you are drinking it.

## How to Prepare Haiiba Timam Tea

1. Use a small thermos flask that holds 2–3 cups.
2. Put one tablespoon of the tea into the flask.
3. Boil 3 cups of water, pour it in and close the lid tightly.
4. Leave it to steep for 30 minutes.
5. Drink all of it within 4 hours.

Drink it **twice a day**: in the morning on an empty stomach, and in the evening before or after a meal. One 200 g packet lasts **7 days**. For all four problems, **5 consecutive weeks** is usually enough, so a full course is **5 packets**.
