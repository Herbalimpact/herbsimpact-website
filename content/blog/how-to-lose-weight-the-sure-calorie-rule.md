Excess body weight is linked to many health problems, so learning how to lose weight is about far more than looks. These problems include:

1. High blood pressure (hypertension)
2. Diabetes
3. Heart disease
4. Bone and joint disease
5. Severe heartburn
6. And more

![Celery wrapped in a measuring tape, showing how to lose weight with vegetables](/assets/uploads/how-to-lose-weight-kupunguza-uzito-celery-measuring-tape.jpg "Filling most of your plate with vegetables is the foundation of healthy weight loss. Photo: Anna Tarazevich / Pexels")

For someone carrying a lot of extra weight, taking steps to shed the excess kilos is not only important for improving appearance. It is also an act of protecting good health, and life itself.

## The Principle That Governs Body Weight

Once the food we eat has been processed by the body, it releases calories (a measure of energy) that power the body's many activities.

Any calories beyond what the body needs are stored as fat, to be used later when they are needed.

## How to Lose Weight Starts With Choosing Your Food

The first thing needed to prevent large weight gain is to make sure we do not take in more calories than the body needs. To achieve this, choosing what we eat matters a great deal.

1. Foods rich in sugar and starch usually bring far more calories into the body than it needs, leading to weight gain, so we should eat them with caution.
2. Protein foods need a great many calories to be processed, more than the calories they themselves provide. This means eating them helps a lot in reducing body weight.

## Body Weight Is a Matter of Maths

**When fewer calories come into the body than go out, weight goes down.**

**When more calories come into the body than go out, weight goes up.**

## Don't Forget the 80/20 Rule

Whatever kind of food we eat, we should not forget the 80/20 rule. This rule says that 80% of the food on your plate should be a mix of different vegetables, and if possible 75% of those should be vegetables that can be eaten raw.
