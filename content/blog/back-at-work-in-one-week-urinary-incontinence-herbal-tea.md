## Urine Leakage Is More Common Than People Admit

Urinary incontinence means losing control of your bladder, so urine leaks when you do not want it to. It affects men and women of all ages. Many people hide it because they feel ashamed, and that silence makes life harder.

Common types include:

- **Stress incontinence:** urine leaks when you cough, laugh, lift or run.
- **Urge incontinence:** a sudden, strong need to pass urine, and you cannot reach the toilet in time.
- **Overflow incontinence:** the bladder does not empty fully, so urine dribbles out. In men this is often linked to the prostate.

You can read more in our article [Urinary Incontinence: Types, Causes and What Actually Helps](https://herbsimpact.com/blog-9.html).

## Please Read This Before You Start

Daniel's story is one patient's experience, not a clinical trial. Every body is different, and results vary.

- **See a doctor first** to find the cause of the leaking.
- **Go to a hospital the same day** if you have blood in your urine, pain or fever, or you cannot pass urine at all.
- **Keep taking any medicine** your doctor prescribed, and tell your doctor you are drinking the tea.

## How to Prepare Haiiba Timam Tea

1. Use a small thermos flask that holds 2–3 cups.
2. Put one tablespoon of the tea into the flask.
