Heartburn is a feeling of severe pain, like burning, in the food pipe, running from the chest up to the throat. Here is what causes heartburn, and why severe heartburn should never be ignored.

![Man pressing one hand on his chest and the other on his upper stomach, showing where heartburn is felt](/assets/uploads/what-causes-heartburn-kiungulia-chest-pain.jpg "Heartburn is felt as a burning pain from the chest up to the throat. Photo: Anastasia Shuraeva / Pexels")

## What Causes Heartburn?

Heartburn happens when acid in the stomach flows upward towards the throat.

This happens mostly when the muscle that closes and opens this passage, located where the food pipe joins the stomach, fails to close when it should, or does not close completely, 100 percent.

This muscle is supposed to open when we eat and drink, and to close when we are resting between meals and drinks.

## Home Remedies for Mild Heartburn

If the heartburn is not very severe, among the home remedies that can bring relief are licking sugar or ash.

## The Dangers of Severe, Long-Lasting Heartburn

However, if heartburn is very severe and keeps coming back, it is wise to seek treatment, and to do so quickly.

Severe, long-lasting heartburn does not only spoil the joy of life to a great extent; it is also one of the risk factors for cancer of the food pipe.
