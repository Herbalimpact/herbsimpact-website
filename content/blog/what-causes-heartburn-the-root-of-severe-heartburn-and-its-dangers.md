Heartburn is a feeling of severe pain, like burning, in the food pipe, running from the chest up to the throat. Here is what causes heartburn, and why severe heartburn should never be ignored.

![A man holding his chest and stomach, a sign of what causes heartburn discomfort](/assets/uploads/what-causes-heartburn-kiungulia-man-holding-chest.jpg "Heartburn is felt as a burning pain from the chest up to the throat. Photo: Anastasia Shuraeva / Pexels")

## What Causes Heartburn?

Heartburn happens when acid from the stomach flows upward towards the throat.

This mostly happens when the muscle that closes and opens this passage, located where the food pipe joins the stomach, fails to close when it should, or does not close 100% of the way.

This muscle is supposed to open when we eat and drink, and to close when we are resting between meals and drinks.

## A Simple Home Remedy for Mild Heartburn

If the heartburn is not very severe, among the home remedies that can bring relief are licking sugar or ash.

## Why Severe, Long-Lasting Heartburn Is Dangerous

However, if the heartburn is very severe and keeps coming back, it is wise to seek treatment, and to do so quickly.

Severe, long-lasting heartburn does not only spoil your enjoyment of life to a great degree; it is also among the risk factors for cancer of the food pipe.
