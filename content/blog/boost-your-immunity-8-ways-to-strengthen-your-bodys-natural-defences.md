## Your Body's Immune System

Our Creator has given us protection through what we call the immune system.

Without it, our lives would probably end shortly after birth, or we might never be born at all.

If we want to keep living in good health and wellbeing, we need to take deliberate steps to look after this system and make sure it always works at its best.

## Eight Steps to Take

The steps include:

1. Eating the right diet;
2. Getting enough sleep;
3. Managing stress effectively;
4. Keeping your body well hydrated at all times;
5. Getting enough sunlight every day;
6. Staying physically active every day (avoiding a sedentary lifestyle);
7. Clearing toxins from the body, both those produced by the body's own chemical breakdown and build-up, and those that come in through food, drinks and contact with the environment; and
8. Taking steps to repair worn-out cells.
