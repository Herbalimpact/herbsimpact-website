According to the World Health Organization (WHO), non-communicable diseases kill an average of 41 million people around the world every year, making them the number one cause of death worldwide. The good news is that the causes of non-communicable diseases are within our power to control.

![A stressed man at his office desk, a picture of one of the causes of non-communicable diseases](/assets/uploads/causes-of-non-communicable-diseases-stress-at-work.jpg "Work stress and long hours of sitting are among the causes of non-communicable diseases. Photo: Yan Krukau / Pexels")

## What Are Non-Communicable Diseases?

Non-communicable diseases include:

1. Diseases of the blood circulation system (heart disease, high blood pressure and stroke);
2. Cancer;
3. Diabetes;
4. Kidney disease;
5. Respiratory diseases;
6. And others.

## The Main Causes of Non-Communicable Diseases

WHO names the main causes of non-communicable diseases as:

1. Tobacco use;
2. A sedentary lifestyle;
3. Harmful use of alcohol;
4. An unhealthy diet; and
5. Breathing polluted air.

## Stress Is a Major Cause Too

Although stress is not mentioned on the WHO list, according to research carried out in Germany, which gave rise to what is called "German New Medicine", stress is among the very major causes of NCDs.

## Understanding Is Our Protection

Fortunately, all the causes of NCDs are within our power to control. What causes the problem is a lack of understanding.

Taking deliberate steps to build understanding is our protection. It is important to attend various trainings on health and wellness, and to read expert writings and the results of various studies.
